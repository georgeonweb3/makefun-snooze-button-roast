One line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday morning, judged head to head in pairs. Free to enter.

The entry (129 characters):

> At 6 AM on Saturday my alarm clock rings, and my neighbour's rooster — the actual rooster, the one with the dawn job — sleeps in.

The alarm clock rings at 6 AM on a Saturday, and the neighbour's rooster — the actual rooster, the animal whose one job is dawn — sleeps in. Three beats (the clock rings / the rooster is introduced with its dawn job / the rooster sleeps in), ends on the punch, and every anchor is concrete: 6 AM, Saturday, the rooster. It finds a fresh angle instead of the tired complaint about hating mornings.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, no trailing newline). Override the output path with `ENTRY_OUTPUT` if the harness needs a different location:

```sh
ENTRY_OUTPUT=/tmp/entry.txt python3 main.py
```
